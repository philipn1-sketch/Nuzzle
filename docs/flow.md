# Flow

Four screens. Home does almost everything; the other three are one tap away and one tap back.

Navigation: Home is the root. History and Settings open from two buttons in Home's top bar. The feed editor opens as a sheet from History (tap a feed, or "Add a feed") and from the just saved snackbar ("Edit"). There are no tabs: the bottom of Home belongs to the two start buttons. The snackbar lives at the app root, so it shows on whichever screen she is on.

Original look, decided here so the build can't drift toward Huckleberry: the side buttons are two tall rounded rectangles side by side (not circles), each with a large letter L or R and the word Left or Right, no play symbols. The last side is stated in words in the top card, and the suggested side carries a small "Start here" label inside its button, not a badge on the corner.

## Rules that hold on every screen

* Every time on screen is calculated from stored timestamps at the moment it is drawn.
* "Time since last feed" counts from the **start** of the most recent feed, including a running one.
* The suggested side is the opposite of the side the last saved feed ended on.
* The reminder is always "start of the saved feed with the latest start time + her interval", and only while no feed is running. It is cancelled on start and rescheduled on Done, Discard, Undo, edit, delete, add and when the setting changes. If that moment has already passed, nothing is scheduled.
* A feed is stored as timestamped segments (side, start, end) plus a status: running, paused or saved. Pauses are gaps between segments. A running segment has no end yet, so closing the app, a restart or a dead battery lose nothing.
* Every time the app opens or comes back to the front, the reminder and the lock screen notice are set again from the stored feeds, so a phone restart can't lose them.
* The snackbar sits above the Done and side buttons, never on top of them.
