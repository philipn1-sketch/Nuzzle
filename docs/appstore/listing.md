## Review notes (for Apple, English)

Nuzzle needs no sign in. Everything works offline on the phone.

To try Nuzzle Plus: every new install gets a free week of Plus automatically, so all Plus screens (Settings, Backup and sharing; Settings, Daily plan; History, Week and Summary; Settings, Another baby) are open during review. The paywall is in Settings, Nuzzle Plus, with Restore, Terms and Privacy links.

Backup and sharing is optional. Settings, Backup and sharing, Start creates a random family code; a second device can join with that code. No personal account is created.

The Apple Watch app and the widget read a summary the phone writes; start a feed on the phone to see them update.

## App Privacy answers

* **Data collection:** Yes, only when the user turns on backup and sharing.
* **Health & Fitness, Other User Content:** collected, linked to the user: No (stored under a random family code, not an identity), used for tracking: No, purpose: App Functionality.
* No contact info, identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, location or purchases are collected by Nuzzle.
