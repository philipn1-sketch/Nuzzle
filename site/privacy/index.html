Privacy policy

The short version: what you log stays on your phone. Nothing leaves it unless you turn on backup and sharing yourself.

Last updated 26 October 2026

Who we are

Nuzzle is made by QuickQuote Group in Australia. You can reach us at admin@quick-quote.com.au.

What Nuzzle keeps on your phone

Your baby's name and birthday, feeds, bottles, nappies, sleep, pumping, weight, length, notes and your settings. These are stored in the app on your phone. We can't see them.

Nuzzle has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. It does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not sell or share data with anyone.

Backup and sharing (optional)

If you choose to start backup and sharing, Nuzzle creates a random family code and sends a copy of your records to our database so a second phone with the same code can see them. This copy includes your baby's name and birthday, the records listed above and the notes you write. It is linked only to the family code, not to your name, email or Apple ID.

The database is run for us by Supabase, which stores the data on our behalf and uses it for nothing else. Anyone who has your family code can see and change these records, so keep it private.

You can delete the online copy at any time in Nuzzle: Settings, Backup and sharing, Delete the online backup. It is removed for good, for every phone using that code. The records on your phone are not affected. You can also email us to have it deleted.

Reminders, widgets and Apple Watch

Feed reminders are scheduled on your phone. The widget, the lock screen timer, the Apple Watch app and Siri read a small summary that stays on your devices.

Purchases

Nuzzle Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your card or Apple ID details. The app only learns whether Plus is active.

Children

Nuzzle is for parents and carers. It is not meant to be used by children, and the information about your baby is entered and controlled by you.

Your choices

  • Delete any record in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on your phone.
  • Stop sharing on a phone, or delete the online backup, from Settings.
  • Ask us what we hold for a family code, or ask us to delete it, at admin@quick-quote.com.au.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
