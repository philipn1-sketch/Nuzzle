# Ring

The big circle in the middle of Home that answers "how long since the last feed" at a glance. 14 thick on a faint track. At rest it is lilac and fills (`progress` 0 to 1) as time passes towards her reminder; while feeding it is a full circle in the feeding side's colour. Put the answer text inside as children.

`size` is the diameter (about 300 on Home), `width` the stroke, `color` and `track` from the palette.
