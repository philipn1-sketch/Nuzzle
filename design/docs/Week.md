# Week

The History screen's Week tab: one chart of the last seven days, oldest at the top and Today at the bottom, with every feed laid on a 24 hour strip. Left (rose) is the tall mark and Right (peach) the short one, so they read apart without colour; bottles are thin ink lines. Under the strips: 12am, 12pm, 12am, and today's totals. Pass the feeds, the current time and the units.
